🔐 Data Security Roles

Understanding the key roles and responsibilities in data protection and cybersecurity governance

👆 Click on any role below to learn about their responsibilities and typical organizational assignments
👑
Data Owner
Senior executive responsible for data classification, governance policies, and strategic oversight of specific datasets.
🛡️
Data Custodian
Technical role responsible for implementing security controls, data storage, and maintaining infrastructure security.
⚙️
Data Processor
Third-party entity that processes personal data on behalf of the data controller according to specific instructions.
📊
Data Steward
Ensures data quality, accuracy, and compliance with governance policies through day-to-day data management activities.
🎯
Data Controller
Determines the purposes and means of processing personal data, legally responsible for GDPR compliance.
👤
Data Subject
Individual whose personal data is being collected, processed, or stored by an organization.

💡 Understanding these roles is essential for implementing effective data governance and cybersecurity programs
