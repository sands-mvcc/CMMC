Compliance Remediation Management & Progress Tracking — Student Practice Exercise
🎓 Student Instructions
  • Begin by filling in the Organization Information fields below.
  • Use + New POA&M Item to add remediation items for identified security gaps — each item requires a Control ID, weakness description, priority, owner, and due date.
  • Track progress by editing items and updating their status (Open → In Progress → Completed).
  • The Dashboard Metrics and Analytics panels update automatically as you add items.
  • Use 💾 Save (or Ctrl+S) to preserve your work in the browser, or Export to download files.
  • When finished, use 🖨️ Print / PDF to submit your completed POA&M tracker.
