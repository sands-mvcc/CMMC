Federal Food Services Inc. (FFSI) CMMC Data Governance Role Assignment Exercise Estimated Time: 30 Minutes Learning Objectives By the end of this activity, participants will be able to: Define the six primary data governance roles used in CMMC environments. Distinguish between responsibility for data ownership, protection, processing, and compliance. Analyze an organizational structure and determine appropriate role assignments. Justify cybersecurity role assignments based on job responsibilities. Explain how proper role assignment supports the protection of Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). Key Terms Term Definition Data Owner Individual accountable for a set of data and who determines how it is used. Data Custodian Individual responsible for maintaining and protecting data systems. Data Processor Individual who uses or processes data as part of their job duties. Data Steward Individual responsible for ensuring data quality, integrity, and proper handling. Data Controller Individual or organization that determines why and how data is processed. Data Subject Person whose information appears within organizational systems. CUI Controlled Unclassified Information. FCI Federal Contract Information. Scenario Federal Food Services Inc. (FFSI) manufactures Meals Ready-to-Eat (MREs) and supplies food products to the U.S. Army. The company stores and processes: Federal contract information Supplier records Employee information Production specifications Shipping and logistics data Quality assurance reports Army delivery schedules FFSI is preparing for a CMMC Level 2 assessment. Your consulting team has been asked to review the company's personnel roster and assign appropriate data governance roles. Directions Step 1 Review the FFSI personnel list provided in the case study. Focus on the following positions: CEO COO CIO Contracts Manager Compliance Manager Production Manager HR Director Logistics Manager Quality Assurance Manager IT Manager System Administrator Cloud Administrator Help Desk Manager Step 2 For each data governance role below, identify the employee who should be assigned responsibility. Roles to Assign Data Owner Data Custodian Data Processor Data Steward Data Controller Data Subject Step 3 Provide a brief justification (1–2 sentences) explaining why each individual is the best choice. Which role created the most debate? How could incorrect role assignments create CMMC findings? What evidence would an assessor request to verify these assignments? Expected takeaway: Successful CMMC implementation requires clear accountability for CUI. Organizations must be able to identify who owns data, who protects it, who uses it, who governs it, and whose information is being stored and processed. This accountability is a key component of effective cybersecurity governance and CMMC readiness.
