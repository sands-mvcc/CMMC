Scoring Legend

Fully Implemented: Control is fully in place — positive points awarded (varies by control weight)
Partially Implemented: Control is partially in place — 0 points (no credit, no penalty)
Not Implemented: Control is not in place — negative points assessed (required in POA&M)

Note: Point values follow the DoD NIST SP 800-171 Assessment Methodology. High-priority controls (weight 5) affect the score by +5/−5. Standard controls (weight 1) affect by +1/−1. Total possible: +110. Total possible deficit: −203.
