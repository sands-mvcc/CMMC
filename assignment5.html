Introduction — Maintenance Documentation and CMMC
🔧
NIST SP 800-171 Domain MA (Maintenance) requires organizations to maintain and control maintenance on organizational systems. Every firmware update, vendor repair, emergency patch, and OS upgrade that touches a CUI asset must be authorized, documented, and reviewed. During a CMMC Level 2 assessment, a C3PAO will ask: Who did this? When? Was it authorized? Was CUI at risk? If you cannot answer those questions with evidence, the maintenance event becomes a finding.
Why Assessors Focus on Maintenance Records
  • Maintenance activities can introduce unauthorized changes, malware, or misconfigurations
  • Unescorted vendor access to CUI systems violates NIST 800-171 practice 3.7.4
  • Emergency changes made without approval create undocumented security posture changes
  • Maintenance logs serve as the audit trail that validates SSP control implementation claims
  • Missing records suggest the organization doesn't control its own security environment
NIST SP 800-171 Maintenance Practices
  • 3.7.1 — Perform maintenance on organizational systems
  • 3.7.2 — Provide controls on tools used for maintenance
  • 3.7.3 — Ensure equipment removed for maintenance is sanitized
  • 3.7.4 — Check media containing diagnostic programs for malicious code
  • 3.7.5 — Require MFA for remote maintenance sessions
  • 3.7.6 — Supervise maintenance activities of personnel without required access authorization

Learning Objectives
1
Identify which FFSI maintenance activities fall within the scope of NIST SP 800-171 Maintenance requirements and explain why each is relevant to CMMC Level 2 assessment.
2
Classify maintenance events as planned or unplanned, internal or external, and authorized or unauthorized — determining whether CUI systems were potentially affected.
3
Complete a Cybersecurity Maintenance Report by filling in missing fields using FFSI case study data — including technician identity, approval status, CUI impact, and documentation status.
4
Analyze vendor maintenance activities to determine whether third-party access was properly authorized, monitored, documented, and compliant with NIST 800-171 practice 3.7.6.
5
Identify maintenance compliance weaknesses and develop corrective actions with risk ratings that reflect the impact on CUI security and CMMC assessment readiness.
6
Prepare a concise executive maintenance summary that answers key questions about documentation completeness, vendor management, and compliance risk for FFSI leadership.
