Network Architecture & Segmentation

Network Segmentation Overview

ACME implements a segmented network architecture to isolate CUI processing from other business operations.

CUI Zone

Secure access to servers, cloud backups, and contract management systems

Management Zone

Administrative and IT operations with controlled access to CUI systems

Guest Network

Completely isolated from internal systems for visitor access

OT Network

Dedicated operational technology for building control systems

Network Security Controls

Protection
