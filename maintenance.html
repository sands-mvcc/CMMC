Highest-priority findings

Maintenance log

One record per work order. Assessors sample these records, so capture who did the work, who supervised, which tools were used, and how remote sessions and off-site equipment were handled.

Maintenance controls

The suggested result comes from the log and findings. Set the final status yourself after examining the evidence and interviewing the staff.

Maintenance personnel

Anyone who performs maintenance. People without the required access authorization must be supervised by someone who has it (MA.L2-3.7.6).

Maintenance vendors

Third parties that service in-scope equipment on site, remotely, or at a depot.

Approved maintenance tools

Software, hardware, and diagnostic media used for maintenance. Software and media must be checked for malicious code before use (MA.L2-3.7.4).

Findings

Checks run whenever the log, tools, or personnel change. Dismiss a finding once it is resolved or formally accepted.

Maintenance program

The written program the log is measured against. Changes save automatically.

Attestation

An internal attestation for this report. It is separate from the annual CMMC affirmation a senior official submits in SPRS.

Settings

Changes save automatically. The reporting period sets the range of the dashboard and the printed report.

A blank report keeps personnel, vendors, tools, and the schedule, and clears the log, risks, and attestation, so students can build the record from a scenario.
