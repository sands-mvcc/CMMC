Why POA&Ms Are Critical to CMMC Preparation

A Plan of Action & Milestones is the primary mechanism through which a CMMC Level 2 organization demonstrates active management of cybersecurity deficiencies. A C3PAO assessor will request the POA&M as evidence that FFSI knows what its vulnerabilities are, has assigned accountability for each, and is tracking remediation with realistic timelines. An organization without a POA&M has no documented pathway to compliance — and no defense against an assessor's finding of systemic negligence.

How POA&Ms Support Continuous Improvement

NIST practice 3.12.2 requires developing and implementing plans of action to correct deficiencies and reduce vulnerabilities. The POA&M is not a one-time artifact — it must be updated continuously as findings are remediated, new vulnerabilities are discovered, and assessment readiness evolves. Assessors will look for version history and evidence of regular review cycles.
