🎯 Tabletop Exercise Scenarios

Add, review, and practice incident scenarios with your team. Each scenario should prompt discussion of detection, response, containment, and reporting actions.

Scenario 1: CUI Data Breach via Email

A contract coordinator reports that they accidentally sent a file containing CUI to an unauthorized external email address.

Discussion Points: CUI classification procedures, immediate containment steps, DIBNet 72-hour reporting requirement, affected party notification, evidence preservation.

Priority: CRITICAL | NIST Reference: IR.L2-3.6.1

Scenario 2: Ransomware Attack on CUI Systems

Multiple workstations in the CUI zone are displaying ransomware messages. The primary data server appears to be affected and encrypted.

Discussion Points: Network isolation procedures, backup integrity validation and restoration, law enforcement notification (FBI), business continuity activation, DoD reporting obligations.

Priority: CRITICAL | NIST Reference: IR.L2-3.6.1, IR.L2-3.6.2

Scenario 3: Insider Threat — Terminated Employee

SIEM alerts indicate unusual after-hours access to CUI systems by an employee who was recently terminated but whose accounts were not fully deprovisioned.

Discussion Points: Emergency access revocation, forensic evidence preservation, HR and legal coordination, scope of potential CUI exposure, investigation procedures.

Priority: HIGH | NIST Reference: IR.L2-3.6.1, AC.L2-3.1.1
